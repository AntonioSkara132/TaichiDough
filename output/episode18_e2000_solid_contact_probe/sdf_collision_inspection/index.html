Definitions and limitations

  • Negative signed distance: inside the collision solid. Positive: exterior. Positive distance is not automatically non-contact when padding is positive.
  • Visual geometry can differ from the watertight collision solid intentionally.
  • Depth exports contain dough only; they do not show tool occlusion and cannot establish collision.
  • Voxel resolution and reconstruction settings affect the displayed SDF.
